# Photos

Real photos from the store's own public Facebook and Instagram (@iamsirgamesalot), used in this mockup. The raw pulls
and post captions are in `assets/` (not shipped). Everything under `public/images/` is served from the site itself, so the
CSP stays `img-src 'self'` and nothing is hotlinked.

The site never uses artwork, box art or logos from Games Workshop, Wizards of the Coast, Blizzard, Riot or any other
publisher as a picture. Some store photos show the shop's own shelves, statues and sign, which carry third-party art;
that is listed in `docs/CONTENT_TODO.md`.

All photos are registered in `lib/content/photos.ts` (alt text, which ones show people). Width, height, blur placeholder
and dominant colour are generated by `node scripts/make-images.mjs` into `lib/content/photo-meta.generated.ts`. Run it
again after adding or replacing a photo. It also crops the baked-in letterbox bars off two social exports and rebuilds
`og.jpg`.
